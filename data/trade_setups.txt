XAUUSD Trade Setup Examples

Setup 1: Valid Bearish Setup

Market conditions:

4H:
The market is showing a bearish structure with lower highs and lower lows.

1H:
Price retraces into the 0.382–0.5 Fibonacci zone of the bearish impulse.

15M:
Price moves above a recent high and then rejects the level, creating a liquidity sweep.

15M:
Price subsequently breaks a relevant swing low, creating bearish Break of Structure.

5M:
Price retraces back toward the relevant area.

5M:
Bearish confirmation appears.

Decision:

The setup satisfies the preferred bearish sequence.

4H bearish
→
1H Fibonacci zone
→
15M liquidity sweep
→
15M bearish BOS
→
5M retest
→
5M bearish confirmation

This can be considered a SELL setup according to the strategy, provided risk management conditions are also satisfied.

---

Setup 2: Invalid Bearish Setup

Market conditions:

4H:
Bearish trend.

1H:
Price reaches the Fibonacci zone.

15M:
No liquidity sweep occurs.

15M:
No bearish Break of Structure occurs.

Decision:

There is insufficient confirmation.

The trader should not enter a SELL simply because price reached the Fibonacci zone.

---

Setup 3: Early Entry

Market conditions:

4H:
Bearish trend.

1H:
Price reaches the Fibonacci zone.

15M:
Liquidity sweep occurs.

However:

15M bearish BOS has not occurred yet.

Decision:

The setup is incomplete.

Do not enter simply because the liquidity sweep occurred.

Wait for the required structural confirmation.

---

Setup 4: Poor Risk-to-Reward

Market conditions:

The technical setup is valid.

Entry:
3500

Stop Loss:
3490

Potential Take Profit:
3505

Risk:
10 points

Potential Reward:
5 points

Risk-to-Reward:
1:0.5

Decision:

The technical setup may be valid, but the risk-to-reward is poor.

The trade should generally be avoided unless the trading plan explicitly allows such a setup.

Setup 5: Bullish Setup

4H:
Bullish market structure.

1H:
Price retraces into the 0.382–0.5 Fibonacci zone.

15M:
Price sweeps liquidity below a recent low.

15M:
Price breaks a relevant swing high.

5M:
Price retests the relevant area.

5M:
Bullish confirmation occurs.

Decision:

The preferred bullish sequence is complete.

4H bullish
→
1H Fibonacci zone
→
15M liquidity sweep
→
15M bullish BOS
→
5M retest
→
5M bullish confirmation

This can be considered a BUY setup according to the strategy, provided risk management conditions are satisfied.
