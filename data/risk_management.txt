XAUUSD Risk Management Rules

Risk management is a critical part of the trading strategy.

1. Risk Per Trade

Risk should be defined before entering a trade.

The trader should know:
- Entry price
- Stop loss
- Take profit
- Position size
- Maximum amount that can be lost

Never increase position size simply because a trade looks highly likely to succeed.

2. Stop Loss

Every trade should have a predefined invalidation level.

For a SELL setup:
- The stop loss should be placed at a technically meaningful level where the bearish setup would be invalidated.

For a BUY setup:
- The stop loss should be placed at a technically meaningful level where the bullish setup would be invalidated.

Avoid moving the stop loss farther away simply to avoid taking a loss.

3. Risk-to-Reward Ratio

The preferred minimum risk-to-reward ratio is 1:2.

Example:

If the trade risks $10, the planned reward should ideally be at least $20.

Risk = $10
Potential Reward = $20
Risk-to-Reward = 1:2

A setup with poor risk-to-reward may not be worth taking even if the technical setup looks good.

4. Position Size

Position size should be calculated based on:
- Account balance
- Percentage of account being risked
- Entry price
- Stop loss distance
- Instrument specifications

Do not choose lot size randomly.

5. Trade Management

Once a trade is active:
- Do not make emotional decisions.
- Follow the predefined trade plan.
- Avoid moving the stop loss without a strategy-based reason.
- Avoid increasing risk after entering.
- Consider partial profit-taking only if it is part of the trading plan.

6. Maximum Risk

Avoid taking multiple trades that collectively expose the account to excessive risk.

Correlated positions should be considered as part of the total portfolio risk.

7. Losses

A losing trade does not automatically mean the strategy failed.

Evaluate whether:
- The setup followed the strategy.
- The entry was valid.
- Risk management was followed.
- The trade was affected by unexpected market conditions.

Never increase risk simply to recover a previous loss.
