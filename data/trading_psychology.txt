XAUUSD Trading Psychology

Trading psychology is an important part of following a systematic trading strategy.

1. Avoid FOMO

Do not enter a trade simply because price is moving quickly.

If the setup has already moved without providing the required entry conditions, wait for another opportunity.

Missing a trade is better than entering an invalid setup.

2. Avoid Revenge Trading

After a losing trade, do not immediately enter another trade simply to recover the loss.

The next trade should be evaluated independently.

3. Avoid Overtrading

A trader does not need to trade every market movement.

Only take setups that satisfy the defined strategy.

No valid setup means no trade.

4. Follow the Process

The goal is not to win every trade.

The goal is to consistently execute the trading plan.

A valid trade can lose money.

An invalid trade can make money.

The quality of the decision should be evaluated separately from the outcome.

5. Do Not Chase Price

If price has already moved significantly away from the planned entry area, do not chase the move.

Wait for a new setup or a proper retracement.

6. Accept Losses

Losses are part of trading.

A predefined stop loss should be accepted as part of the risk-management process.

Do not move the stop loss simply because you do not want to accept the loss.

7. Maintain Discipline

Before entering a trade, confirm:

- Higher timeframe direction
- Fibonacci zone
- Liquidity sweep
- Break of Structure
- Retest
- Confirmation
- Risk-to-reward
- Position size
- Stop loss
- Take profit

If the required conditions are not satisfied, do not force the trade.
